Share Your PCs

What do I need?

  • You must have Laplink Everywhere 1.20 or higher installed on your host computer. Click the Laplink Everywhere client icon on your system tray and select the Help->About Laplink Everywhere menu item to find out which version you have. The latest version is:  Laplink Everywhere, v3.0

How do I set up sharing?

  1. On your host computer, click on the Laplink Everywhere client icon (blue swirl) in your system tray and select the "Configure->Users and Sharing..." option.


  2. Use the "Privileges" configuration window that appears, to add users to your list of "Users".
  3. Use the tabs on the right to configure what folders and access rights each particular user has.


  4. Save your configuration settings by choosing the "OK" or "Apply" button.
  5. That's it! When the user logs into the http://rome.infowave.com site they will see your shared Laplink Everywhere information available on the left side navigation bar of this site.

What if the person I want to share with does not have a Laplink Everywhere username?

  • They may create a Laplink Everywhere username by completing the short sign-up form here

Why would I use the sharing feature?

  1. Share file folders with co-workers to be more productive by sharing common documents.
  2. Share customer contacts with co-workers to improve communications with customers.
  3. Share calendars with co-workers to be more efficient scheduling internal meetings.
