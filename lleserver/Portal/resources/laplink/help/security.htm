When I access my stuff at https://www.ll2go.com, what stops a third party from seeing my information as it travels over the Internet?
The Laplink Everywhere service supports 128 bit end-to-end encryption. We leverage HTTPS to securely transmit your information over the Internet, using Secure Socket Layer (SSL) encryption.

This is the same technology used by major e-commerce sites to securely transmit credit card information over the Internet. You can verify that you have end-to-end encryption by confirming that your browser is showing a small yellow padlock in the lower right area.
What keeps a third party from hacking your servers to access my information?
We NEVER store any of your emails, contacts, appointments, or files on our servers; instead, our servers simply request and format the information directly from your PC each time you access https://www.ll2go.com to request something.

We do store your Laplink Everywhere password on our servers and have implemented multiple levels of security to protect your password...

1) Passwords are stored using non-reversible MD5 encryption (even our internal staff cannot access passwords)

2) Our servers are located in a high security data center with restricted physical access based on biometric hand scanners

3) High security servers with restricted internal access based on security policy

4) Failed logins have a long delay which makes it difficult for someone to try lots of passwords quickly
What keeps a third party from bypassing your secure servers and accessing my PC directly?
Laplink Everywhere never accepts socket requests; instead, Laplink Everywhere creates a connection with our servers and only receives information requests from our servers via this connection.

How would I know if someone gains access to my stuff?
To help you monitor the connections made to your computer, Laplink Everywhere records detailed information about all:

-Connection Requests
-Active Connections
-E-Mail Activities
-E-Fax Activities
-File Transfers
-Processed Forms
-Database Requests

To access and view the information, open Laplink Everywhere, click View Connections or View History in the console tree, and then select your area of interest. The entries appear in the console pane.

If I use https://www.ll2go.com at a public terminal, what keeps a third party from accessing my stuff by pressing "Back" on the browser after I leave?
We turn page caching off. If you've logged out and someone presses "Back" on the browser after you leave, they will be unable to view the page without knowing your user name and password.
