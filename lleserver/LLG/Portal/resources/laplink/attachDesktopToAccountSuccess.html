You can now use the "Connect over Laplink Internet" feature in Laplink Gold 12 to access this PC.

To use Laplink Internet feature:
1. Start Laplink Gold 12 on another computer.
2. Select "Connect over Laplink Internet".
3. Enter your Laplink Internet username and password.
4. Select the name you gave this PC from the list of PCs available to you and the services you wish to use.
5. Click "OK" to make a connection to that PC.
